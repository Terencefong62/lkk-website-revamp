Leading Change

Four change pillars focused on transformation and impact.

Vision-driven

  • Envision the future by presenting an ambitious and inspiring vision along with ground-breaking strategies
  • Exert influence by sharing the vision and goals to turn stakeholders into advocates for change
  • Maintain optimism and confidence despite resistance and obstacles, steadfastly driving the vision and goals forward

User-centric

  • Always approach from the users' perspective to gain a deep understanding of their diverse needs
  • Take swift action to address and resolve users' pain points and issues
  • Commit to delivering unique value to users and consistently exceed their expectations

Demonstrate Agility & Adaptability

  • Believe that "The only Constant is Change", lead by example, and proactively embrace and adapt to change
  • Maintain a clear direction and make decisive, informed decisions even in uncertain situations
  • Encourage others to embrace a trial-and-error approach and continuous upgrading through iteration, and to learn effectively from failures and mistakes

Strive For Excellence

  • Demonstrate a passion for excellence and maintain strong confidence in the undertaking one does
  • Set high goals and standards for oneself and others, and embrace challenging tasks
  • Consistently display a "can-do" attitude and action orientation, facing challenges head-on and taking proactive steps
