Autopilot Leadership

Six leadership dimensions guiding daily execution.

Choosing The Right Talent
  • Attract outstanding talents who are both culture fit and competent
  • Recognize and utilize people's strengths, placing each person in a position where they can excel
  • Have the courage to employ people who are more capable than yourself, continuously increasing the talent density
High Trust Environment
  • Trust others first, believing that everyone is trustworthy
  • Respect everyone's opinions and consider their perspectives
  • Practice active listening, express openly, speak the truth, and foster an atmosphere of mutual trust
Highly Effective Team
  • While helping others, also actively seek help, fostering collaboration and resource sharing
  • Be willing to take on extra responsibilities for the team and honor commitments to the team
  • Address issues directly and ensure they are brought to the table and ensure the focus is on the issue, not the person
Common Goal
  • Actively invite relevant individuals to participate in discussions and decision-making, leveraging collective wisdom and efforts
  • Utilize effective models and tools to facilitate open, efficient, and productive discussions
  • Establish clear and mutually understood common goals through open communication and consensus
Effective Empowerment
  • Clarify the objectives of delegated tasks, define the scope of work, and specify the desired outcomes
  • Ensure that employees have the resources and support they need to achieve their goals
  • Clearly define employees' responsibilities and provide timely follow up and feedback
Coaching and Developing Talent
  • Encourage everyone to learn from others, learn through doing, and learn through teaching, achieving mutual growth through teaching and learning
  • Provide timely affirmation, recognition, and praise to empower employees and boost their confidence
  • Coach and guide employees with care and patience to unlock everyone's potential

Vision-driven

  • Envision the future by presenting an ambitious and inspiring vision along with ground-breaking strategies
  • Exert influence by sharing the vision and goals to turn stakeholders into advocates for change
  • Maintain optimism and confidence despite resistance and obstacles, steadfastly driving the vision and goals forward

User-centric

  • Always approach from the users' perspective to gain a deep understanding of their diverse needs
  • Take swift action to address and resolve users' pain points and issues

Demonstrate Agility & Adaptability

  • Believe that "The only Constant is Change", lead by example, and proactively embrace and adapt to change
  • Maintain a clear direction and make decisive, informed decisions even in uncertain situations
  • Encourage others to embrace a trial-and-error approach and continuous upgrading through iteration, and to learn effectively from failures and mistakes

Strive For Excellence

  • Demonstrate a passion for excellence and maintain strong confidence in the undertaking one does
  • Set high goals and standards for oneself and others, and embrace challenging tasks
  • Consistently display a "can-do" attitude and action orientation, facing challenges head-on and taking proactive steps
