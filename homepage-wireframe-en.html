Module 3 · Our Story · Expand

Know us · Where we came from

It began with an unexpected discovery.

In 1888, Lee Kum Sheung invented our oyster sauce after a pot of oyster soup simmered longer than intended. From that single invention, Lee Kum Kee has grown across generations by responding to the evolving tastes and cooking needs of consumers and chefs around the world. Today, more than 300 sauces and condiments bring Lee Kum Kee to home and professional kitchens across over 100 countries and regions—continuing a story of craftsmanship, constant entrepreneurship and culinary progress.

Module 6 · Our Commitments · Merge

Believe in us · What we stand behind

Quality without compromise. Growth with responsibility.

Our commitments are rooted in quality without compromise and growth with responsibility. Our 100-1=0 zero-tolerance quality philosophy guides every step from farm to fork. Guided by Si Li Ji Ren—considering others' interests—we also work to protect product integrity, reduce environmental impact, care for our people and contribute to communities as we pursue more sustainable growth.
