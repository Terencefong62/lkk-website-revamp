Our Story

It began with an unexpected discovery.

In 1888, Lee Kum Sheung invented oyster sauce after a pot of oyster soup simmered longer than intended. From that single invention, Lee Kum Kee has grown across generations—bringing craftsmanship, constant entrepreneurship and culinary progress to home and professional kitchens in over 100 countries and regions.
