Homepage Module 3 · Our Story

It began with an unexpected discovery.

In 1888, Lee Kum Sheung invented oyster sauce after a pot of oyster soup simmered longer than intended. From that single invention, Lee Kum Kee has grown across generations by responding to the evolving tastes and cooking needs of consumers and chefs around the world. Today, more than 300 sauces and condiments bring Lee Kum Kee to home and professional kitchens across over 100 countries and regions—continuing a story of craftsmanship, constant entrepreneurship and culinary progress.
