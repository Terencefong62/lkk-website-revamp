About Us

A Century of Craft, Quality, and Entrepreneurship

Since its founding in 1888, Lee Kum Kee has been part of cherished moments around family tables across generations. Built on Hong Kong roots and guided by enduring values, we carry forward traditional craftsmanship while constantly innovating for modern kitchens.

We believe every great flavor can connect people. That belief drives our commitment to consistency, food quality, and cultural exchange through Chinese culinary excellence.
